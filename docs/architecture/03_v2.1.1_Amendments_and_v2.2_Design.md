## Part 1 — v2.1.1 Amendments (Cleanup Only)

These amendments fold into v2.1 without reopening the architecture. P0A scope remains unchanged in function.

### A1. Explicit `user_id` on all P0A tables

```sql
projects (
  id, user_id NOT NULL,           -- FK to auth.users
  name, employer, role_at_time, start_date, end_date,
  description, business_context, my_scope, team_size, stakeholders[],
  privacy_class, status, created_at, updated_at
)

achievements (
  id, user_id NOT NULL,
  project_fk, headline, narrative, action_taken, outcome,
  start_date, end_date, claimed_seniority_level,
  truth_status, privacy_class, has_metric_bool,
  candidate_for_external_bool,    -- renamed per A3
  status, created_at, updated_at
)

metrics (
  id, user_id NOT NULL,
  achievement_fk, metric_name, value, unit, time_period,
  baseline_value, calculation_notes,
  truth_status, privacy_class, status, created_at
)

evidence_items (
  id, user_id NOT NULL,
  achievement_fk, type, content_summary, external_url,
  privacy_class, verified_at, verified_by,
  status, created_at
)
```

RLS policies enforce `user_id = auth.uid()` on all reads, writes, and deletes.

### A2. Lifecycle status fields

| Table | Status values | Default |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| projects | `active`, `archived` | `active` |
| achievements | `draft`, `active`, `archived` | `draft` |
| metrics | `active`, `archived` | `active` |
| evidence_items | `active`, `archived` | `active` |

**Rationale:** evidence backfill creates weak entries, duplicates, and partial drafts. Archive/draft replaces deletion; nothing is destroyed, just hidden from default views.

### A3. Rename `approved_for_external_bool` → `candidate_for_external_bool`

P0A captures user *intent* that an achievement might be used externally. Final approval is enforced by P0B (truth/privacy gates) and P0E (per-asset version approval). The rename removes false finality.

### A4. Infrastructure language

Replace "Supabase project provisioned" with **"Supabase project configured or reused; migrations apply cleanly on the target instance."**

Reuse the existing Supabase account/project where possible to avoid sprawl. New project only if there's a clean reason (e.g., entirely separate billing isolation).

### A5. Gate 1–4 market-motion menu — sharpened

Update §14 of v2.1 with explicit allowed/disallowed lists for low-risk relationship maintenance:

**Allowed at Gates 1–4:**
- Thoughtful comments on contacts' posts
- Congratulations on promotions, launches, anniversaries
- Non-job-seeking check-ins ("how's the new role treating you")
- Sharing useful public content with attribution
- General professional engagement (event attendance, public discussion participation)
- Public LinkedIn brand-building posts (not job-shopping in tone)
- Public-source archetype research and JD review
- Story-bank refinement against archetypes
- Comp benchmark research from public sources (Levels.fyi, published reports)

**Disallowed at Gates 1–4:**
- Referral asks of any kind
- Recruiter outreach (inbound recruiters may be politely deferred)
- "I am looking" / "open to opportunities" language anywhere external
- Formal applications
- Active interview pursuit
- Any signal of intent to leave current employer that could surface in monitoring

The app's action queue surfaces only Allowed actions at these gates; Disallowed items remain dormant in the system until later gates unlock them.

### A6. Director-signal threshold — tightened

Update §9 of v2.1:

> An achievement is "director-signal" when it scores **≥4 on at least 4 of the 7 dimensions, AND scores ≥4 on both Cross-functional Influence AND Scope/Scale, AND scores ≥3 on Ownership.**

Ownership becomes a floor condition. Director-level credit cannot accrue to achievements where ownership was peripheral, even if other dimensions are strong.

### A7. JD retention policy

Update §8 of v2.1:

- **Raw pasted JD text** in `archetype_sources.raw_content`: retained 90 days post-archetype-approval. After 90 days, auto-purged unless the source record is manually pinned.
- **Parsed summaries** in `archetype_sources.parsed_summary`: retained indefinitely.
- **User-pinned** raw JDs: retained indefinitely until manually unpinned.

Rationale: JDs go stale fast and retaining raw scraped text indefinitely creates needless surface area. Parsed summaries are the durable artifact.

## Part 2 — v2.2 Design (P0A Experience, No Functional Expansion)

### Goal

Make P0A feel like a **serious private career evidence cockpit** — dense, deliberate, information-rich — not a generic SaaS form-filler. The user should sense from the first screen that this is a tool for serious career capital construction, not a notebook.

### Non-Negotiables

v2.2 must not introduce:
- AI behavior (suggestions, autocomplete, summaries)
- File uploads or storage
- Gmail / Calendar / LinkedIn integration
- Visa Decision Checklist UI
- Achievement Grader or any scoring logic
- Career Asset Generator
- Target Archetypes
- Sunday Review / Friday Capture (the *cadence* — not its UI)
- Sanitized Claim Builder

What v2.2 *does* introduce: visual language, navigation, capture flow, empty states, density, and the cockpit feel — all on top of P0A's locked four-entity schema.

## 3. Visual Direction

### Reference aesthetic

**Yes:** Linear (density and craft), Sublime Merge (precision), Things 3 (typographic respect), terminal-influenced power-user tools.

**No:** Notion (consumer pastels), generic SaaS dashboards, marketing-driven UI, anything with gradient backgrounds, anything that looks like it was made for screenshots.

### Typography (lock now)

- **Sans body:** Inter or IBM Plex Sans
- **Monospace:** JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono — used for: metric values, dates, IDs, status badges, table data, anything numeric
- **Headlines:** Same sans as body, with strict weight/size hierarchy (no decorative display fonts)
- **Maximum 2 typefaces in the entire product.**

### Color (lock now)

- **Base:** zinc / slate neutrals. Dark mode default; light mode secondary.
- **Semantic accents only:**
  - Truth status: blue spectrum (VERIFIED = strong, NEEDS_PROOF = muted/warning)
  - Privacy class: green (PUBLIC_SAFE), amber (INTERNAL_ONLY), red border / muted background (PRIVATE)
  - Lifecycle status: full opacity (active), reduced opacity (archived), italic/dashed border (draft)
- **No decorative color.** No purple, no teal, no gradients, no brand colors.

### Density principles

- High information density per screen. Whitespace is deliberate, not generous.
- Tabular layouts preferred for vault views.
- Detail pages: information-rich, scannable, no marketing-style hero sections.
- No mascots, no illustrations, no emoji in product copy.
- Tone of copy: clinical, precise, second-person. "Add evidence to this achievement" not "Let's strengthen your story! ✨"

### Lockable in hours, not weeks

These choices are stylistic constraints, not a design system to be built from scratch. Tailwind CSS + a few component patterns (shadcn/ui as starting point) executes this language in P0A's build window.

## 4. Information Architecture

### Primary navigation (left rail or top, three sections only)

1. **Vault** — projects, achievements, metrics, evidence (core P0A surface)
2. **Pipeline** — drafts, recent activity, archived items (the working layer)
3. **Settings** — profile, defaults, data export, RLS audit log

That's it. No additional top-level navigation. Future slices add modules; they do not add new primary nav until earned.

### Vault hierarchy

```
Vault
└── Employer (auto-grouped from projects.employer)
    └── Project
        └── Achievement
            ├── Metrics (nested)
            └── Evidence Items (nested)
```

### Detail pages

Achievement detail is the **central object** of the cockpit. The screen layout:

- **Header:** Headline, employer/project breadcrumb, lifecycle status badge, truth status badge, privacy class badge, candidate_for_external_bool toggle
- **Narrative panel:** Action taken, outcome, business context — markdown-rendered
- **Metrics panel:** Tabular, monospace values
- **Evidence panel:** List with type icons, content summary, link to external if present
- **Metadata footer:** Dates, claimed_seniority_level, created/updated timestamps
- **Future-slice placeholders:** A single "Future surfaces" footer strip indicates where Grader score, Sanitization status, and Asset references will appear — labeled clearly as future, not as broken or missing features

### Pipeline view

A working-layer view distinct from Vault:
- **Drafts** queue — incomplete achievements awaiting promotion to active
- **Recent activity** — last 20 entries across all entities
- **Archived** — soft-deleted items, browsable, restorable

This view is where the user lives during evidence backfill. Vault is where polished, active material lives.

### Global filters (P0A scope)

Available across Vault and Pipeline:
- Lifecycle status
- Truth status
- Privacy class
- Employer
- Date range
- `candidate_for_external_bool`

No full-text search in P0A (deferred — see §7 buckets).

## 5. Capture Flow (Backfill-Optimized)

The single most important UX in P0A. The user will spend hours mining 3+ years of work. Friction here kills momentum and the product fails.

### Quick Log pattern

- **Cmd/Ctrl + N** opens an inline capture surface (not a modal blocking the screen).
- Required fields only: headline + project. Everything else defaults or is optional.
- Submit creates achievement in `status = draft`, `truth_status = NEEDS_PROOF`, `privacy_class = INTERNAL_ONLY`.
- After submit: capture surface stays open, focus returns to headline. Batch entry is the default mental model.

### Progressive enhancement

A draft achievement can be created in 5 seconds (just a headline). Promotion to active requires:
- Narrative
- At least one of: metric OR evidence_item
- Privacy class affirmed by user (not default)
- Truth status moved off NEEDS_PROOF (or user explicitly confirms it remains)

The UI surfaces what's needed to promote without nagging. No red errors; calm gray prompts.

### Inline editing

All achievement fields (and child metrics/evidence) are editable inline on the detail page. No "Edit" mode toggle. Click-to-edit, blur-to-save, autosave indicator in the corner.

### Keyboard-first

- `N` — new achievement in current project context
- `E` — edit focused field
- `Esc` — blur and save
- `J/K` — navigate up/down in lists
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Enter` — promote draft to active (if requirements met)
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + A` — archive
- Shortcuts surfaced in a `?` help overlay; never required, always available

### Mobile capture

On phone:
- Single-column layout
- Headline + project picker = the entire required surface for Quick Log
- Larger touch targets
- Thumb-zone primary action button (bottom-right floating)
- Detail edits still inline, optimized for one-handed scrolling

The mobile experience is for capture, not authoring. Long-form narrative editing is desktop-primary by design.

## 6. Empty States (Cockpit-Grade, Not Marketing-Grade)

Empty states are where most products betray themselves with marketing copy and illustrations. v2.2's empty states are functional, specific, and director-level in tone.

### Vault empty (first launch)

> Your evidence vault is empty.
>
> Start by logging one project from your current role at DIRECTV. A project is a body of work — a system you built, a problem you owned, a function you led. Achievements come next, inside projects.
>
> Press **Cmd+N** or **Add Project**.

(No illustration. No "Welcome!" No emoji.)

### Project with no achievements

> No achievements yet for **[Project Name]**.
>
> Achievements are discrete outcomes within this body of work. Examples: "Reduced churn forecast error by 18%", "Launched the executive retention dashboard", "Led the migration from SAS to Python."
>
> Add the first one — Cmd+N.

### Achievement with no metrics

> No quantified impact yet for this achievement.
>
> Director-level achievements typically carry at least one metric — revenue, retention, cost, scale, time, accuracy. If the impact wasn't measured, note that explicitly in the narrative so future grading reflects it accurately.

### Achievement with no evidence

> No evidence linked.
>
> Without evidence, this achievement remains NEEDS_PROOF and cannot be promoted to external use. Evidence can be a document reference, a link, an email reference, a peer testimonial, or a metric source. Even a one-line note about where the proof exists strengthens the record.

### Pipeline empty

> No drafts. No recent activity.
>
> The Pipeline view shows your working layer — incomplete achievements, recent edits, and archived items. As you log evidence, this is where you'll triage and refine.

### Archived empty

> Nothing archived.
>
> Archive is for items you've decided not to use but don't want to lose — weak achievements, duplicates, exploratory drafts. Nothing here is deleted.

## 7. Enhancement Classification

Per the v2.2 brief, every enhancement classified into one of four buckets.

### Design-Now (ship with P0A)

- Typography lock (Inter + JetBrains Mono, or IBM Plex pair)
- Color system lock (zinc/slate base + semantic accents only)
- Three-section navigation (Vault, Pipeline, Settings)
- Vault hierarchy with employer grouping
- Achievement detail page layout
- Pipeline working-layer view
- Empty states as drafted in §6
- Quick Log capture pattern with Cmd+N
- Inline editing with autosave
- Keyboard shortcuts (`N`, `E`, `Esc`, `J/K`, Cmd+Enter, Cmd+Shift+A, `?`)
- Mobile capture optimization
- Global filters (status, truth, privacy, employer, date, candidate_for_external_bool)
- Lifecycle status visual treatment (active full, draft dashed/italic, archived muted)
- Privacy class color encoding
- Truth status badging
- Settings panel: profile, defaults, data export, RLS audit log
- Future-surfaces footer strip on achievement detail (labeled as future, never as broken)
- Dark mode default; light mode secondary

### P0A-Visible (UI scaffolding for future, no functionality)

- Future-surfaces footer strip mentioned above (placeholders only, no behavior)
- `candidate_for_external_bool` toggle (writable in P0A; consumed by P0E later)
- Truth status / privacy class display patterns (consumed by P0B enforcement later)
- Vault summary counters in Settings (counts by employer / status / privacy / truth — pure data display, no AI, no grading)

### Deferred (later slices)

- Full-text search across vault entities (defer to P0G or later)
- Bulk operations on selections (defer — careful design needed with truth/privacy enforcement)
- Duplicate detection hints (likely AI-assisted; defer to a later slice)
- Markdown preview / split view (defer; basic markdown render is enough in P0A)
- Inline timeline view of an employer's projects/achievements (defer; vault list is enough)
- Drag-to-reorder achievements within a project (defer)
- Tag system beyond status/privacy/truth (defer; over-engineers backfill)
- Mobile native app (deferred indefinitely; responsive web suffices)

### Rejected (will not build)

- Any AI-driven UX in P0A (suggestions, autofill, smart anything)
- Onboarding wizard with marketing tone
- Illustrations, mascots, decorative imagery
- Gamification (streaks, points, badges, "you've logged N achievements 🎉")
- Social / sharing / collaborative features
- Public profile views or shareable links to vault content
- Loading animations implying AI progress (since there is no AI in P0A)
- "Smart" anything — smart compose, smart suggestions, smart anything
- Notifications, push, email digests of vault activity (defer all to P0G's brief generator)
- Theming / customization (one cockpit aesthetic, locked)

## 9. Risks v2.2 Introduces

To be intellectually honest:

1. **Design polish can delay build.** Mitigation: lockable in hours of decisions, not weeks. If v2.2 review is taking longer than two days, it's over-designed.

2. **Cockpit aesthetic may feel cold during heavy backfill.** Mitigation: empty states are encouraging without being saccharine; capture flow is fast enough that the user doesn't dwell in friction.

3. **Future-surfaces footer may confuse users.** Mitigation: clearly labeled as "future surfaces (not yet active)". Removable if it becomes noise during P0A use.

4. **Keyboard shortcuts overhead.** Mitigation: shortcuts are always optional; mouse/touch paths exist for everything. Power-user feel without power-user requirement.

5. **Dark mode default may not survive light mobile environments.** Mitigation: respects system preference, light mode functional.

## 10. Open Questions for ChatGPT Review on v2.2

1. Is the "no AI in P0A" purity actually right, or should P0A include some local (non-LLM) helpers like auto-grouping by employer or duplicate-headline warnings?
2. The future-surfaces footer strip — does it create useful anticipation, or risk feeling like vaporware on day one?
3. Is `candidate_for_external_bool` as a P0A-visible toggle right, given that its enforcement doesn't exist yet? Could it create false confidence?
4. Mobile capture is positioned as capture-only, not editing. Is that too restrictive for a user who's likely to do real work between meetings on phone?
5. Cmd+N and the keyboard-first patterns — overkill for a single-user tool, or essential to the cockpit feel?
6. The empty-state copy is intentionally director-grade in tone. Is it cold? Is it accurate to how the user actually thinks?
7. Should Pipeline be a distinct section, or just a saved filter on Vault?
8. Is one cockpit aesthetic (no theming) too rigid?
9. Anything in Deferred that should be promoted to Design-Now?
10. Anything in Rejected that should be reconsidered for P0A scope?
